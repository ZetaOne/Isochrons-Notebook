# Isochrons-Notebook

**The website: https://zetaone.github.io/Isochrons-Notebook/**

A guided reading of H. M. Osinga and J. Moehlis, *Continuation-based computation of global
isochrons*, SIAM J. Appl. Dyn. Syst. 9 (2010) 1201-1228 ([the paper](https://sites.me.ucsb.edu/~moehlis/moehlis_papers/isochrons.pdf), on the
authors' site; it is linked here, not copied).

## What is on the site

- **The book**, *Isochrons: Where Phase Lives*: 18 chapters from the first ideas (phase,
  clocks, isochrons) to the paper's continuation method, to read in the browser or as a PDF.
- **Slide decks** for 15 of the chapters; each remembers the slide you stopped at.
- **The code** behind every figure of the book, to read or download.
- **A study kit**: the paper explained section by section, a recipe of the computation, course
  scripts and notebooks, and XPPAUT shell and scripting examples, each next to its chapters.
- **Your reading**, kept in your own browser only: highlights in five colours with notes, where
  you stopped, your progress in each deck, and the display (theme, text size, font).
  Nothing is sent anywhere.

Every simulation is computed by Bard Ermentrout's
[XPPAUT](https://sites.pitt.edu/~phase/bard/bardware/xpp/xpp.html) through
[pyauto-xpp](https://zetaone.github.io/pyauto-xpp/); the models can also be explored in the browser with
[XPPAUT-Web](https://zetaone.github.io/XPPAUT-Web/).

## About this repository

The files here are generated: they are rebuilt from the book's sources and the study material,
so changes go into those, not into these files. GitHub Pages publishes the `main` branch.

This site was called **notebook** until 27 November 2026 (then at zetaone.github.io/notebook/).
